---
title: Monochrome
summary: ESP32-S3 firmware that sends a 720x350 picture to a TTL monochrome monitor.
---

Monochrome is ESP-IDF firmware for an ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 (N16R8). It drives a TTL monochrome monitor of the [[term:MDA]] or Hercules type through a DE-9 connector. The firmware draws a calibration pattern with two brightness levels, and you replace it with your own image.

Start with [[start/build-and-flash]].
