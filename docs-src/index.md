---
title: Monochrome
summary: ESP32-S3 firmware that sends a 720x350 picture, including a real-time 3D demo, to a TTL monochrome monitor.
---

Monochrome is ESP-IDF firmware for an ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 (N16R8). It drives a TTL monochrome monitor of the [[term:MDA]] or Hercules type through a DE-9 connector. The firmware includes a shaded 3D torus knot that runs at 49 frames per second, a wireframe screensaver and a calibration pattern, all drawn with three brightness levels.
