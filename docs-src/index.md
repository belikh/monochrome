---
title: Monochrome
summary: ESP32-S3 firmware that shows frames sent over Wi-Fi, and a real-time 3D demo, on a TTL monochrome monitor.
---

Monochrome is ESP-IDF firmware for an ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 (N16R8). It drives a TTL monochrome monitor of the [[term:MDA]] or Hercules type through a DE-9 connector. The firmware joins Wi-Fi and shows frames that a program on your network sends over TCP or UDP, with optional compression. It also includes a shaded 3D torus knot that runs at 49 frames per second, a wireframe screensaver and a calibration pattern, all drawn with three brightness levels.
